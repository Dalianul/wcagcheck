What this checks, and what it can't

Why EN 301 549 and not just WCAG

The Accessibility Act doesn't name WCAG directly. It points at the European standard EN 301 549, whose clause 9 restates the WCAG Level A and AA criteria for websites. Every finding here shows both references.

Static analysis only

This reads your page's HTML. That catches missing alt text, unlabelled form fields, broken heading order and disabled zoom. It cannot judge colour contrast or keyboard traps, which need a real browser to measure.

Not a legal audit

A clean result here means the machine-detectable problems are gone. Roughly a third of accessibility requirements need a human to assess. Treat this as triage that tells you where to start.
